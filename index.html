Online Consultation System

PythonC++Healthcare-tech
Presented · Paper published

A platform built to connect patients with doctors remotely, replacing long queues and rigid clinic hours with a simple, accessible booking and consultation flow. I designed and built it end to end, then documented it formally as a research paper and a project presentation.

  • Limited access to timely medical advice, especially outside clinic hours
  • Long waiting times and avoidable in-person visits for minor concerns
  • No simple, structured way for patients to consult a doctor remotely
  • Mapped the end-to-end user flow — from booking to consultation record
  • Implemented the core system logic and data handling in Python
  • Structured the underlying data model for patients, doctors, and sessions
  • A working prototype of the full consultation flow
  • Presented to faculty as a formal minor project
  • Written up and published as an academic research paper
